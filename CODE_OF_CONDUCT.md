# Code of Conduct

Pi Bot adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone taking part in the project's issues, pull requests,
discussions and other spaces.

In short: be respectful and considerate, welcome people of every background and level of
experience, give and accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the community.
Harassment, personal attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not
acceptable.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub: open an issue at
https://github.com/w01fb0ss/PiBot/issues, or, for anything that should stay private, use the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**) and say it is a conduct report. Reports are
handled privately; the maintainers decide on the response following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
